490-              "><h2>About Mega Man X2</h2>
491:<p><strong>Mega Man X2</strong> is a fast-paced <strong>action-platformer</strong> developed by Capcom and released in 1994 for the <strong>Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES)</strong>. Set in a futuristic world where Reploids and Mavericks threaten humanity, the game continues the story of X, a Maverick Hunter determined to stop Sigma and his forces. Building on the foundation of the original Mega Man X, this sequel introduces new levels, redesigned bosses, and enhanced gameplay mechanics, while keeping the signature mix of precise platforming and challenging combat. Players will experience vibrant stage designs, dynamic boss encounters, and the thrill of upgrading X’s abilities to adapt to ever-tougher challenges.</p>
492-<hr>
493-<h2>How To Play</h2>
494-<p>In <strong>Mega Man X2</strong>, you control X through multiple themed stages, each ending with a boss fight against a unique Maverick. Players navigate intricate platforms, dodge hazards, and engage in fast-paced combat. Unlike the first game, X2 introduces <strong>rideable vehicles</strong> in certain stages, such as the hovercraft for water levels, adding a fresh dynamic to movement. Collecting hidden heart tanks and upgrade capsules is crucial to surviving tougher areas. Defeating bosses grants their weapons, which can be strategically used against other enemies for maximum effect. Timing your dashes, wall jumps, and charged shots is key, and secret paths often reward exploration with powerful enhancements. X2’s levels combine classic precision platforming with new gameplay twists that make mastering each stage a rewarding challenge.</p>
